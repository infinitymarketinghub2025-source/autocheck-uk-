Frequently Asked Questions

Is this real MOT data right now?

Not yet — the report above shows the exact format your report will follow. Live MOT data will be connected soon.

Is any part of this free?

No — the full report is £30 per vehicle, paid once. There's no free tier or trial search.

What information will be included?

Make, model, colour, fuel type, and engine size, plus complete MOT test history: every test date, pass/fail result, mileage reading, failure reasons, and advisory notices on file.

Does this include tax status or finance/stolen checks?

Not currently. This report covers vehicle details and full MOT history only. Tax status and finance/stolen/write-off checks aren't included at this time.
